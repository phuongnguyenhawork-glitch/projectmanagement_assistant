## Tracking plan

The events below are intended to show whether teams can get started, collaborate, and make progress after creating a project. The proposed north-star metric is the number of teams active in a week that submit at least one task or move one task to Done.

### Data minimization

Track only the events needed to improve the product. Avoid collecting brainstorm text, task descriptions, or personal information.

| Event | What it measures | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `signup_completed` | Counts users who finish email sign-in. | Shows whether users can get past the access step. |
| `onboarding_completed` | Counts users who finish initial project setup. | Reveals whether setup is understandable and manageable. |
| `project_created` | Counts projects created. | Indicates whether users bring a real project into the product. |
| `brainstorm_completed` | Counts completed brainstorm sessions. | Shows whether teams complete the guided goal-setting step. |
| `plan_generated` | Counts generated plans containing milestones, WBS items, or tasks. | Measures use of the core idea-to-plan workflow. |
| `member_invited` / `member_joined` | Counts invitations sent and teammates who join. | Measures whether the product supports team adoption, not just individual use. |
| `task_assigned` | Counts tasks assigned to a team member. | Shows whether plans become clear ownership and action. |
| `task_status_changed` | Counts transitions between task states. | Measures task-board use and can reveal where work gets stuck. |
| `task_submitted` | Counts tasks submitted for review. | Tracks work completion and use of the feedback loop. |
| `task_approved` / `task_returned` | Counts tasks approved or sent back for revision. | Shows whether review is happening and how often rework occurs. |
| `meeting_poll_created` / `availability_submitted` | Counts meeting polls and team availability responses. | Measures the usefulness and participation rate of meeting coordination. |
| `weekly_active_team` | Counts teams with at least one task submission or completion in a week. | Shows whether teams return and make progress beyond initial setup. |
